## Tweens church 
## Tweens church 
The children who feel they do not fit in Victory kids church yet feel they are too young for Ark Arise (Youth Service)  are part of the Tweens Church. They are an energetic and enthusiastic bunch of teenagers that can be themselves and study the word of God. They meet every Sunday from 10:15 a.m to 11:30 a.m to read , study and understand the word of God  .

## discipleship 
## The discipleship groups 
Beyond the traditional Sunday services, our church works to create a sense of family through disciple group gatherings. These gatherings provide a space for intimate fellowship, where we collectively study into the enriching teachings of God’s word.  Our discipleship groups are a place where we pray together and study the word together and do life together and stand for each other. It is a nurturing place to grow in the Lord and to experience church being a family.

## Discipleship is the process of following and becoming more like Jesus and is the point of the Christian walk. 
To follow Jesus with immediate and complete obedience, make God first priority in life and follow Jesus with wholehearted commitment and singular focus. 
Discipleship cannot happen just on a Sundays. That is why we meet in smaller groups throughout the city during the week for fellowship and to dive deeper into the questions about Sunday sermons.

## Rakshak 
## One little heartbeat at a time- 
Rakshak is out NGO , founded in 2004, aiming to create an environment for underprivileged children in informal settlements. Over the 19 years, Rakshak has been able to serve 1000 plus children . Our children, by God’s grace have been placed in good positions and good jobs and some of them are in colleges. Our dream is to see the cycle of poverty broken in their life.
