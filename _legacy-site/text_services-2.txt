## Tweens church 
The children who feel they do not fit in Victory Kids yet feel they are too young for Ark Arise are part of the Tweens church. They are an energetic and enthusiastic bunch of teenagers that can be themselves and study the word of God. They meet every Sunday to read , study and understand the Word of God.

## Rakshak 
## One little heartbeat at a time- 
Rakshak is out NGO , founded in 2004, aiming to create an environment for underprivileged children in informal settlements. Over the 19 years, Rakshak has been able to serve 1000 plus children . Our children, by God’s grace have been placed in good positions and good jobs and some of them are in colleges. Our dream is to see the cycle of poverty broken in their life.
