## Dawn service - 5 a.m 
Seek ye first the kingdom Of God – every first day of the month , the church gathers together  to seek the Presence of the Lord for the new month. 
Every first day of the month holds special significance for us as a church community. As the word says, “Seek ye first the kingdom of God,”  we seek the Kingdom Of God and presence and guidance of the Lord for the new month ahead. Through worship, prayer, and fellowship, these gatherings offer us an opportunity to draw closer to God, renew our faith, and set the tone for a spiritually fulfilling and blessed month. It happens every first Sunday of every month at 5 a.m

## Special sundays 
Special Sundays at our church are joyous occasions that allow us to come together as a community and celebrate the diverse and meaningful aspects of our lives and faith. Each of these unique Sundays holds a special place in our hearts and offers us an opportunity to reflect, honor, and rejoice in various important themes and events. 
For instance, Mother’s Day is a cherished day where we express our gratitude and love for all the wonderful mothers and mother figures in our congregation. We take this time to celebrate their unconditional love, sacrifices, and the invaluable role they play in nurturing and shaping our families and communities. 
Valentine’s Day is another beautiful occasion where we celebrate the sacred bond of marriage and love. It’s a time to honor the commitment, companionship, and love shared between couples in our church. We celebrate the beauty of marriage as a divine union and a reflection of God’s love for us. 
Women’s Day is a special Sunday dedicated to recognizing and honoring the incredible women in our church and society. We celebrate their strength, resilience, faith, and contributions to our community and the world. It’s a day to uplift and empower women, acknowledging their unique gifts and the vital role they play in our families, church, and broader society. 
These are just some instances of our  Special Sundays  and these special Sundays are not only occasions of celebration but also moments of reflection and spiritual growth. They remind us of the importance of love, family, community, and faith in our lives.It’s a time for unity, celebration, and renewal as we continue to grow together in faith and love as a church family.
