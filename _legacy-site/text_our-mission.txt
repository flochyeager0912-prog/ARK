## MISSIONS 
We believe that God has called each of us as Christians to be missionaries here in the city of Chennai, reaching our colleagues, friends, family, and neighbors with the good news of Jesus. God also calls us to the “ends of the earth,” or to National and global missions.

### Petras and Rakshak 
ARK Victory church is integrated to Petras and Rakshak – two local missions of the Church.

### purpose 
Petras, founded in 2009, is a Christ-centered business unit of ARK Victory Church. It was formed with the purpose of building lives and raising resources for planting churches across the country.

## MINISTRY TEAMS 
Another great way to get connected is to Volunteer at ARK Victory Church by joining one of our Ministry Teams – serving Jesus in and through practical ways at Church. We have incredible teams of people who serve on ministry teams, with the heart to make Jesus known and to serve each other through the offering of their gifts, time and talents. Ministry Teams are a great way to get involved, meet others within our church body and serve together at ARK Victory Church.
