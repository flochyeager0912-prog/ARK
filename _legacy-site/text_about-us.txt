
###### About Us 
## WHERE CHURCH IS FAMILY 
The Ark VCI is a passionate, word-based church in South Chennai, aiming to build God’s Kingdom through ministering to individuals and families. Our core value is to love God & love people unconditionally which gives the Ark a unique flavour. People of all age groups, languages, ethnicities, and cultures are welcome to our church. At Ark, every individual is encouraged and edified to grow in their calling and be a committed Disciple of Jesus Christ. The Ark VCI’s purpose is to glorify God by establishing a powerful local church, teaching “Transformation of life” through prayer, worship, and fellowship, resulting in Godly characters, disciples, and good works.
